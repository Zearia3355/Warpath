Warpath changelog

=====================================================================
3.8.5 (in progress: test builds)
=====================================================================

The chronicle
- The chronicle is a new book: crimson leather with gold corners, a
  stack of pages, and parchment. Its type is the game's own, with a
  bundled Garamond (free to share, OFL) for italics and figures.
- Each campaign is written up by a chronicler, zone by zone, with a
  route map of where you went, and its deeds and losses on a sheet
  that folds by zone and scrolls. One spread per campaign.
- Quiet nights (patrols with no fighting) can be hidden.
- A new Ledger tab: your whole war in figures, over seven spreads.
  The tally and your records, the hours and the calendar of your
  fights, the classes you slew and that slew you, your lands, your
  foes of note, deaths to the wilds, and your guild's war. Switch
  between this character and all your characters.
- Grudges: one list and one log of every encounter with every grudge,
  newest first, with the chosen grudge's account beside it.
- The Warpath page (this one) shows a key to the map's marks, and
  What's New scrolls on one page instead of turning pages.
- Settings: easier to read, with smaller sub-settings under their
  switch, and a live preview of your kill captions.

On the map
- Corpse runs, flights and spirit healers are now three map layers,
  each with its own switch: in Settings, on the map button's
  right-click, and with /warpath layer runs|flights|spirits.
- Guildmates' kills and deaths and guildmates' sightings each have
  their own switch, under Kill and death pins and Enemy sightings.
  /warpath layer guild still switches both.
- With guild sharing off, guildmates' pins, sightings and battle haze
  are hidden from your map, and their deaths no longer count toward
  dangerous-ground warnings. Nothing is deleted: turn sharing back on
  and they're back.
- Fixed a rare hang while drawing dashed lines on the map (corpse
  runs used the same code).

Settings
- Each length (trail, corpse runs, flights) sits under its layer and
  greys while that layer is off. Clearer wording throughout.
- Grudge messages no longer call your grudges a "ledger".

Guild sharing
- Kill messages now carry the victim's class, for the Ledger.
  Guildmates need the new version for their victims' classes to show;
  kills from older versions show the class as unknown.

- Your saved data: nothing is removed or changed. The new map layers
  start as the old ones were set, and the old switches are kept for
  older versions. Warpath now also counts flights, corpse runs,
  releases at a spirit healer, miles as a ghost, your level and health
  at each kill, and what killed you in the wilds; these start from
  your first fight on the new version.
- Needs a full game restart (new files), not just /reload.

3.8.4
=====================================================================

On the map
- Spirit healers show on zone and city maps, with the corpse runs
  layer, so you can see where you'll come back after releasing. They
  stay faint while you're alive and brighten while you're dead.
- A graveyard only one faction can use carries a small Horde or
  Alliance badge; shared ones have none. Hover one for its name and
  who can use it.
- Warpath starts with the classic graveyards and learns every one
  your characters release to, on any character on your account. A
  graveyard both your Horde and Alliance characters have used counts
  as shared.
- /warpath debug spirits says how close the nearest known spirit
  healer is.

Guild sharing
- When several of you get credit for one kill, its chat line now names
  everyone, not just whoever reported it first: the others are added
  at the end, "(with ...)". A big group shows three names and a
  count ("and 6 others"); the pin's tooltip names them all.
  The line comes about 2 seconds after the kill, to wait for the
  others. Only your copy needs 3.8.4 to see this.

- Your saved data: everything is kept. One new account-wide list of
  the graveyards your characters have released to, and kill pins
  remember who else had credit.
- Needs a full game restart (a new file), not just /reload.

=====================================================================
3.8.3
=====================================================================

On the map
- Scores everywhere. The world map shows one for each continent,
  continent maps one for each zone, and a zone's own map shows its
  score in the top-left corner. Blue when you're ahead, yellow when
  it's close, red when you're behind.
- Where pins crowd on continent and world maps, three or more kills
  or deaths close together become one slightly bigger sword or skull.
  Hover it for how many, and how many were yours.
- Flight paths on Kalimdor and Eastern Kingdoms are thinner and
  smoother, with clean evenly spaced dashes and no doubled lines.
- In a battleground, your other maps still show your war. Only the
  battleground's own map stays clean. The map button, minimap button
  and this book work inside too.

Your trail
- Older trail stays crimson (or your faction's colour) in long dashes
  with a dark edge, so you can follow it hours later even on busy map
  art. Sharp turns stay solid. Where you went the same way twice, only
  the newer pass shows; crossings still show both.
- Along roads you can see, your trail fades away after about 10
  minutes, so the roads read clean.
- Lingering areas are wider in the open world and tighter in cities,
  and areas that touch become one. Past area outlines are drawn like
  the trail around them, and their time labels are yellow and stay
  readable longer.
- Inside the area you're lingering in, and in old areas you walk
  back into, the short tail behind you fades out smoothly like a
  comet instead of ending sharply.
- Leaving an area no longer brings your line back inside it for a
  minute: the area stays clean.

The chronicle
- The book has a Warpath tab: a welcome and What's New. After every
  update the book opens there by itself, with the new release marked.
- Settings live in the book now, on the Settings tab, in plain words,
  with Restore defaults for each section. The separate options window
  is gone. /warpath setup and the minimap button's right-click open it.
- Campaigns list their deeds by zone, and only highlights with
  something to say. Grudges list encounters by day, and a grudge with
  no kills either way says "no blood yet".
- The book's close button works in combat.

Settings that changed
- "Announce on screen" replaces the banner, big-text and quiet-mode
  switches. If you had quiet mode on, announcing, sounds and the line
  under the zone name stay off.
- Guildmates' kill and death lines in chat can be switched off without
  turning off guild sharing.
- Recent flights get their own length on the map, beside corpse runs.
- Guild sharing starts on for new players. Yours stays as you set it,
  and there's a switch for it on the welcome page.

Fixes
- Ground warnings now speak wherever you've fought, so your hunting
  grounds and contested ground get called out too, not only places
  you've died. The setting counts fights (after 1, 2 or 3).
- Ground warnings stay up long enough to read.
- A kill several guildmates get credit for is announced and pinned
  once. Guildmates need 3.8.3 too for this.
- The map button no longer prints a chat line, and hiding Warpath
  with it no longer leaves the last drawing on the map.
- /killshot and /pks no longer work: use /warpath shot.

- Your saved data: everything is kept. Your settings carry over into
  the new switches; nothing else about your data changes.
- No new files: a /reload loads it, but restart the game to see the
  new version number and have the book open on What's New.
- Includes 3.8.0 to 3.8.2.

3.8.0 "Roads, trails and flights"
=====================================================================

- Roads rebuilt. Each way you really travel becomes one road, instead
  of several side by side, and short walked gaps are joined. Roads look
  like raised gold trim: silver first, then a thread of gold along the
  edge, then fully gold once the road is a habit (about 12 days of
  daily use). Your most-worn roads carry a streak of your faction's
  colour. Roads you stop using only fade; they never jump or move.
- Today's trail rebuilt. Where you lingered (questing, a town) becomes
  an outlined area with how long you spent there; hover it for each
  visit. Between areas, the last 2.5 minutes are a solid line, then
  dashes with small arrows showing which way you went, changing colour
  as they age.
- Flight paths: the routes you fly most, on two or more days, show as
  blue dashes. One-off flights don't, so the map never becomes a web.
- Today's trail now stays 12 hours by default (was 1 hour).
- Your map settings are reset once for this release: every map layer
  is turned back on and today's trail is set to 12 hours, so you see
  the new looks. Your icon size, the map on/off switch and corpse-run
  times are kept. Change anything on the Map tab.
- /warpath clear trail clears today's trail and lingering areas,
  keeping everything else.
- /warpath roads now says how long a road you walk every day takes to
  turn gold, and /warpath zone counts roads the way the map draws them
  (gold, turning gold, silver).
- Only your copy changes; nothing shared with guildmates is different,
  and guildmates on 3.7 keep working with you.

- Your saved data: everything is kept. Roads are merged into one road
  per route the first time you log in; your old roads are kept, hidden,
  so nothing is lost (your saved file gets bigger until a later release
  drops them). Lingering areas and flight legs are new and are added
  as you play; your old flight routes are turned into flight legs. Map
  settings are reset once, as above. Don't go back to 3.7.11 or older
  after this: they don't understand the merged roads.
- New files: restart the game fully (a /reload is not enough).
- Includes 3.7.0 to 3.7.11.

3.7.6
=====================================================================

Today's trail: a line, then dashes, then gone
- Your last 5 minutes are a solid line, as before. From 5 to 15 minutes
  it breaks into dashes that get shorter, thinner and fainter, gone by
  15 minutes. Footsteps are gone.
- Every dash keeps its place on the ground: walking on and time passing
  only shorten and fade it. Nothing jumps around any more.
- The "Today's trail" length setting is gone from the Map tab, since the
  trail is now always 15 minutes. /warpath memory <days> still sets how
  fast roads, haze and flight routes fade (the old "<hours> <days>" form
  still works; the hours are ignored).

Your own kills and deaths in chat
- Your kills and PvP deaths now print a chat line, worded exactly as
  your guildmates see it ("Lethality scored a triple kill in Felwood!
  Bloodthirsty: 3 without dying."). On by default; General tab ("My
  kills and deaths in chat") or /warpath chat [on|off]. Works with
  sharing off too.
- In quiet mode this line replaces the old short one, so there's no
  second copy.

Death lines
- Deaths have a dozen ways of being said instead of one: "fell in",
  "bit the dust in", "Felwood claims...", "off to the spirit healer",
  and when the killer is known, "Aldric cut down...", "ran into Aldric,
  and lost", "a new score to settle with Aldric". Taking someone down
  with you and ended streaks get more wordings too.
- Guildmates see the same line you do once they're on 3.7.6. On 3.7.5
  they still get the plain "was killed in" line.

- Your saved data: everything is kept. Each stretch of today's trail
  also saves its distances along the way, so dashes stay put across
  logouts and reloads. Your trail-length setting is kept (unused).
- No new files: a /reload loads it (the version number shown stays
  3.7.5 until you next restart the game).
- Includes 3.7.0 to 3.7.5.

3.7.1
=====================================================================

- Fixed: chat filling with "No player named 'Lethality Starunner-' is
  currently playing". WoW Forever gives names as "First Last" with no
  realm, so Warpath didn't recognise its own guild messages echoing back,
  took itself for a guildmate, and whispered its battle history to a
  misspelt copy of your name, one failed whisper per message. Your own
  messages are now recognised however the name is written, whispers go to
  the name exactly as the game gives it, and if the game says a player
  isn't online, whatever was queued for them is dropped.
- The same echo meant your own kills and deaths could come back as a
  guildmate's (a chat line about yourself, and a second copy in the
  haze). That's fixed too, and 3.7.0's copy of you is removed.
- The battle haze is rebuilt from your kills and deaths only. The old
  haze had enemy sightings mixed in (3.6 counted each a quarter of a
  kill) plus your own echoed fights counted again, so ground where you
  died once and saw a lot of enemies looked like a war zone. In the
  Hinterlands: one death and 24 sightings had become a dark patch; now
  it's one faint patch at your skull.

Your saved data: kept. The old haze is saved alongside, hidden, not
deleted. The only thing removed is 3.7.0's copy of your own fights filed
as if from a guildmate.

Includes 3.7.0.

=====================================================================
3.7.0
=====================================================================

After updating, restart the game: 3.7 adds new files, and a /reload
won't load them.

Multi-kills, streaks and feats
- Kills within 2 minutes of each other make a chain: Double Kill, Triple
  Kill, Quad Kill, then Bloodbath (5 or more). The same enemy twice within
  10 seconds is still one kill.
- Streaks have names: Bloodthirsty (3 without dying), Rampage (5),
  Warmonger (10), Unstoppable (15), Legend of the Warpath (20, then every
  10).
- Feats: Grudge settled, Nemesis down (who killed you most tonight),
  Payback (who last killed you, within 10 minutes), Guardian (the enemy
  who killed a guildmate in the last 2 minutes), Clutch (under 15%
  health), Outnumbered, Underdog (3+ levels above you, or ??), Comeback
  (after 3+ deaths in a row), Back from the dead (within a minute of
  coming back), Death avenged, Old rival (your 10th, 25th, 50th, 100th
  kill of a player), Ground taken (a kill on your dangerous ground) and
  Flawless (at full health).
- One line per kill, for you and your guildmates: the tier, plus the best
  feat or streak milestone it earned ("Bob scored a triple kill in
  Felwood! Bloodthirsty: 3 without dying."). The separate "5 kills
  without dying" line is folded in. The wording varies so chat isn't the
  same line over and over, with a "Blood and thunder!" or "For the
  Alliance!" now and then on streaks of 10 or more.
- Big text on screen for your own multi-kills and streaks, just under the
  kill caption, so the screenshot catches it. A new kill while it shows
  upgrades it in place. General tab: on/off and small, medium or large;
  /warpath overlay [on|off|test]. Quiet mode turns it into chat lines.
- When a streak of 3 or more ends: "Streak ended at 7 by Aldric", small
  and grey.
- Every feat and multi-kill makes the kill notable for screenshots, goes
  on the kill mark, and shows in the chronicle's Deeds list. New
  chronicle highlight: Best burst.
- Guildmates still on 3.6 see one plain line per kill, as before.

Counting kills
- Kills are counted from the game's honorable-kill counter, and an enemy
  seen dying nearby can name a kill. Two kills in one burst are no longer
  lost when you never targeted the second victim.
- Guarded, because it isn't known yet whether this client ever credits
  one death twice: a credit with no name, within 2 seconds of a counted
  kill and with no second death seen, isn't counted.
- /warpath debug kills lists the last credits and what was counted. A
  night of play there shows whether double credits ever happen.

Dangerous ground (replaces killing fields)
- Your PvP deaths are kept on the battle-haze grid, spreading and fading
  the same way. Walk onto ground where they add up and Warpath says what
  that ground has been for you:
  Dangerous ground (red, warning sound): "you've died here 7 times and
  won once."
  Contested ground (amber, softer sound): "20 kills, 20 deaths here."
  Your hunting ground (gold, no warning sound): "12 kills, 3 deaths here."
  Guildmates' deaths there are added on when they count.
- Out of combat only, never in neutral towns, once per area per session,
  and never on top of a kill's big text.
- General tab: ground warnings on screen, in chat, and warn after 1, 2 or
  3 deaths (2 by default). If killing-field warnings were off, these
  start off. /warpath fields on|off still turns both on or off.
- The war table says "Dangerous ground nearby" or "Contested ground
  nearby"; /warpath zone counts dangerous-ground spots.

Guild-wide battle haze
- Guildmates running 3.7 share their battle history: at login Warpath
  asks, and guildmates whisper back what changed since they last sent
  you theirs, a little at a time behind kills and deaths. Their live
  kills and deaths add to it too.
- Each guildmate's history is kept separately and capped: three quarters
  of the strongest guildmate's heat on a spot, plus a little of everyone
  else's, never quite full. Your own fights always read darkest.
- Enemy sightings no longer add to the haze, yours or anyone's: it's
  kills and deaths only. Sightings still show as crests and count in zone
  stats.
- Map tab: "Battle haze" (the whole layer) and "Guildmates' battle haze";
  both also in the map button's right-click menu. General tab: "Share my
  battle history". /warpath share history [on|off]; /warpath share sync
  asks guildmates for everything again; /warpath share status shows it.
- Guild sharing now includes the enemy's name on kill and death lines
  (the kill lines and Guardian need it). Still never your trail.

Map icons
- Kill, death, sighting, guild and grudge pins keep their size on screen
  when the map is enlarged (Leatrix Maps) or zoomed, instead of growing
  with it. Lines, footsteps and the haze still scale with the map.
- Map icon size, 25% to 200%: Map tab, or /warpath icons <25-200>.

Minimap button
- Left-click: the chronicle. Right-click: settings. Drag it around the
  minimap's edge. Greyed out in battlegrounds. Button bags (MBB) and
  Titan Panel pick it up. On by default; General tab or /warpath minimap.

Your saved data: everything is kept. Dangerous ground is built from your
existing death marks; nothing is removed or converted. Your battle haze
so far has old sightings and guildmates' shared fights mixed in that
can't be separated out: it stays as it is and fades with play as always.

Includes 3.6.4 (built, never installed): the battle haze actually shows,
fighting adds up across an area, neutral towns hidden instead of wiped,
/warpath setup as the options command, killing-field warnings on/off,
/warpath debug haze. 3.7.0 changes two of those: guildmates' fights
count three quarters (now capped), and killing-field warnings became
dangerous ground.

3.6.4
=====================================================================

- The battle haze actually shows. It was tuned so faint that a fight
  left nothing you could see: one kill drew at about 9% opacity, and only
  a spot with six or more fights reached full strength (almost none did).
- Fighting now adds up across an area: each kill, death or sighting also
  warms the ground around it, so a fight that moves a few hundred yards
  builds one patch instead of scattering into specks.
- Visible from the first fight: one kill or death leaves a faint red
  patch, a skirmish a clear one, a running battle a dark one. A lone
  enemy sighting still leaves nothing.
- Guildmates' kills and deaths count three quarters of yours (was half).

- Guildmates' kills and deaths no longer go missing. A message that came

  fight. Shared sighting crests fade over their 15 minutes like your own.

  your zone as your zone.
- Guildmates need 3.6.1 too: the fix is on the receiving end.

Neutral towns

  neutral towns, enemy players aren't recorded as sightings: no crests,

- /warpath debug says whether Warpath thinks you're in a neutral town.

War table
- "4 enemies seen (5 min)" instead of a line cut off by the skull button.

Grudges

=====================================================================
3.4.0
=====================================================================

The chronicle
- /warpath chronicle opens a book of your campaigns: one per page, the last
  30 kept per character. Each page has a headline ("The Hillsbrad Skirmish
  - 9 kills, 3 deaths"), kills, deaths, best streak, miles and time played,
  six highlights (nemesis, favourite victim, bloodiest ground, longest
  streak and who ended it, grudges settled and deaths avenged, screenshot
  numbers) and every kill and death. Click one to set a waypoint there.
- A campaign is play with gaps under 2 hours: a /reload or a quick relog no
  longer splits a night in two, and empty logins don't push out real ones.
- The nemesis is the enemy you were fighting when you fell. The game doesn't
  say who landed the blow, so a third player's kill is credited to them.
- At login, a one-line headline for the campaign that just ended (option),
  or the window itself (option, off by default).
- /warpath chronicle chat prints the full report to chat instead.
- Share a campaign: the Share button, or /warpath chronicle share
  [guild|party|say|whisper <name>]. Two plain chat lines anyone can read.
- /warpath chronicle preview shows the window with sample data.

Zone records
- Entering a zone you have history in: a chat line (kills, deaths, miles,
  enemies sighted, deaths avenged, best streak there, and a tag such as
  "your deadliest zone") and a short line under the big zone name. Each can
  be turned off on its own. Again after 30+ minutes away.
- Enemy busy hours are gone from zone messages and /warpath zone.

The map
- A button in the world map's top-right corner (crossed swords): left-click
  hides or shows everything Warpath draws, right-click picks layers,
  shift-drag moves it. Recording never stops.
- Roads gild gradually: silver, warm silver, pale gold, light gold, gold.
  Well-worn gold gets a warm centre stripe, a bronze edge, a slow shimmer
  (can be turned off) and a few amber glints.
- Footsteps are about a third smaller; fresh ones are vivid with a thin dark
  rim for their first 10 minutes.
- The solid line covers your last 5 minutes and fades along its length into
  the footsteps. Fixed: it was often much shorter than intended after you
  stood still.
- Corpse runs and flights have their own layer ("runs"): spirit runs are
  dashed ice blue with a soft glow, flights thin, soft gold dashes. They fade
  along their length and stay 24 hours (1 to 48, in options).
- Flight routes you keep taking (about five separate days within a couple
  of weeks) stay on the map as faint, sparse dashes, a little stronger with
  use, fading over weeks if unused.

Commands and options
- /warpath help lists the everyday commands; /warpath help all lists
  everything, grouped. Unknown commands get a one-line hint, not a dump.
- Every on/off setting takes on or off (or toggles with neither).
- New names: map (was toggle), table (was hud), waypoint death|kill (was
  revenge / lastkill), options (was setup), layer trail / guild / runs. The
  old names still work.
- /warpath clear all asks you to confirm.
- The options window has tabs: General, Map, Screenshots, Chronicle & zones.
- The map on/off switch is shared by all your characters.

Under the hood
- Split into several files. After updating, restart the game (a /reload
  isn't enough the first time).
- Kill marks also store tag, streak and times killed (for a future
  screenshot renamer).
- Saved data upgrades itself; trail recorded before 3.4 shows as footsteps.

=====================================================================
EVERYTHING SINCE 3.2 (the 3.3 series, up to 3.3.5)
=====================================================================

Kill screenshots (replaces the PvPKillShot addon)
- Every kill in the open world gets a screenshot, or only notable kills:
  first kill of a player, grudges, avenged deaths, streaks of 3, 5 and every
  10, anyone you've killed 5+ times.
- A small caption in the shot: floating text (default), a one-line caption
  strip, or none. Banners and sounds wait until after the picture.
- Hover a blade on the map to see its screenshot number and file name.
- /warpath shot (settings, test). /killshot and /pks still work once
  PvPKillShot is removed; Warpath warns you if both are installed.

Kills and deaths
- One enemy death is one kill, however many honorable-kill credits the game
  gives for it. One death is one death, even if the game reports it twice.
- Kills only count if you were in combat (or left it in the last 3 seconds),
  so a groupmate's kill while you stood by isn't yours.
- Victims are named reliably in combat: Warpath reads what the game still
  allows, remembers players it has seen as enemies, and falls back to the
  last enemy you targeted in the past 15 seconds.
- PvP deaths are recognised in combat: an enemy you targeted recently, or a
  non-group player on a nameplate when you fall.

The map
- Silver roads: a route you've used on two separate days. Gold from about
  the third day, thicker with use. Both fade over weeks if abandoned.
- Roads have a dark outline so they show on light and dark zones.
- Today's trail is footsteps that start bright and fade over 30 minutes to a
  faint floor, held until the day's memory expires. A comet tail follows you.
- The solid line now covers only your last 2 minutes (corpse runs and flights
  keep 20).

Guild sharing
- Shares your kills as well as sightings and PvP deaths.
- Allies' kills and deaths in your zone print a chat line; sightings are
  map-only.
- Kills, deaths and test pings go out first, and the queue drains every second.
- /warpath share test sends a test ping; /warpath share status shows sent,
  refused and received counts.

Battlegrounds and arenas
- Warpath is completely off inside: no recording, banners, sounds,
  screenshots, guild messages, war table or map drawing. Detection uses several
  signals and re-checks every 5 seconds. Everything returns when you leave.

Setup and settings
- A setup window opens once after each install or update with the key
  settings; /warpath setup reopens it. Escape doesn't count as seen; if it
  fails to open, the error is printed in chat.
- These settings are shared by all your characters.
- /warpath debug shows what Warpath can and can't see.
