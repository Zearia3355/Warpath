Warpath changelog

=====================================================================
3.6.4
=====================================================================

- The battle haze actually shows. It was tuned so faint that a fight
  left nothing you could see: one kill drew at about 9% opacity, and only
  a spot with six or more fights reached full strength (almost none did).
- Fighting now adds up across an area: each kill, death or sighting also
  warms the ground around it, so a fight that moves a few hundred yards
  builds one patch instead of scattering into specks.
- Visible from the first fight: one kill or death leaves a faint red
  patch, a skirmish a clear one, a running battle a dark one. A lone
  enemy sighting still leaves nothing.
- Guildmates' kills and deaths count three quarters of yours (was half).
- Your existing history is redrawn the new way straight away.
- /warpath debug haze lists this zone's strongest haze spots.
- Neutral towns are hidden, no longer wiped. Haze and enemy sightings in
  Booty Bay, Gadgetzan and the like still don't show on the map or in zone
  stats (enemies are expected there), but what was recorded is kept
  instead of erased the first time you walk through. (Anything 3.6.1 to
  3.6.3 already erased can't be brought back.)
- /warpath setup is the name for the options window again (/warpath
  options still works).
- Killing-field warnings ("you have died here N times") can be turned
  off: options window, General tab, or /warpath fields off. On by default,
  as before.
- Your saved data: everything is kept. Nothing is removed or converted;
  this release only changes how the haze is drawn and what towns show.
- Includes everything from 3.6.1 to 3.6.3.

=====================================================================
3.6.3
=====================================================================

- Today's trail stays readable. Footsteps used to fade to a faint 18%
  within half an hour and sit there, almost invisible, for the rest of the
  trail's life. Now they fade slowly over the whole trail length: with a
  24-hour trail, 90% after an hour, 75% at 6 hours, 55% at 12, 35% at 18,
  gone at 24. Shorter trails fade the same way, compressed.
- Older footsteps deepen to a darker faction colour (crimson for Horde,
  deep blue for Alliance) between 1 and 2 hours, so they don't wash out to
  pink or pale blue on light ground. Every step is still drawn.
- Unchanged: the solid line for your last 5 minutes, and the vivid
  footsteps with a dark rim for the first 10.
- Includes everything from 3.6.1 and 3.6.2.

=====================================================================
3.6.2
=====================================================================

- Guildmates' kills and deaths print a chat line wherever they happen
  ("Bob scored a kill in Felwood."), not only in your zone. Sightings
  stay map-only.
- /warpath share status shows the last guild kill or death received.
- Both of you need 3.6.2: the chat line is decided on the receiving end.

=====================================================================
3.6.1
=====================================================================

Guild sharing
- Guildmates' kills and deaths no longer go missing. A message that came
  within 3 seconds of that guildmate's previous one was thrown away, so a
  kill right after a sighting (most kills) was lost, chat line and all.
  Now only exact duplicates (the same message by guild and by party) are
  dropped, and kills and deaths always get through.
- A second kill or death in the same spot within a minute is now shared
  too (only sightings are held to one a minute per spot).
- Guildmates' kills and deaths are drawn above sighting crests, a little
  bigger, so a sword is no longer hidden under the crests of its own
  fight. Shared sighting crests fade over their 15 minutes like your own.
- The "scored a kill / was killed" chat line counts a cave or town inside
  your zone as your zone.
- Guildmates need 3.6.1 too: the fix is on the receiving end.

Neutral towns
- In Booty Bay, Gadgetzan, Ratchet, Everlook, Light's Hope and other
  neutral towns, enemy players aren't recorded as sightings: no crests,
  no war table count, no haze, nothing shared, no zone-stat count. Kills,
  deaths and grudge alerts still work there.
- The haze and sighting counts that had piled up over a town are cleared
  as you walk through it, and guildmates' sightings from there are ignored.
- /warpath debug says whether Warpath thinks you're in a neutral town.

War table
- "4 enemies seen (5 min)" instead of a line cut off by the skull button.

=====================================================================
3.6.0
=====================================================================

- Battle haze: the map hazes where fighting happens, in one red that
  deepens as fights pile up. Your kills and PvP deaths count fully, a
  guildmate's shared kill or death half, an enemy sighting a quarter. It
  fades over 14 days you play, like roads, and is drawn so it can't wash
  out to white. One skirmish leaves a faint smudge; a warzone a clear patch.
- It replaces three things: the lingering haze (where you stood about is no
  longer recorded), the sighting dots, and the killing-field glows. The
  "you've died here N times" warning, skulls, blades, live sighting crests
  and grudge pins are unchanged.
- Your existing kills, deaths and sightings are already in it on first load.
- Layer names: "battle" (old name "heat" still works), "sightings" now
  means the live crests of recent sightings.

=====================================================================
3.5.1
=====================================================================

- The chronicle's Campaigns and Grudges tabs stand on the book's top edge,
  styled like the title plate, instead of hanging off its right side.
- Page turning is a quiet "Earlier" / "Later" with the spellbook arrows at
  the bottom corners. Share is a small button beside "Deeds and Losses".
- The campaign write-up runs straight into its numbers and highlights (no
  big gap), without the boxed initial. "Grudges and vengeance" is now
  "Vengeance" so it no longer runs into the next line.
- The login options are no longer repeated in the book (they're in the
  options window, Chronicle tab).
- Ledger: "Inscribe a name..." sits under the summary; "you · them" heads
  the tallies; Waypoint and Forgive sit under the grudge's note.
- New: a skull button on the war table. Click it to hold a grudge against
  your target (or the player last offered in chat); right-click opens the
  ledger. It replaces "Inscribe my target" in the book.
- Your own realm name is no longer shown on every grudge.
- The ornaments are small red diamonds (the star drew as a box).
- Campaign write-ups no longer count a continent as a zone you rode through.

=====================================================================
3.5.0
=====================================================================

Grudges
- A ledger of grudges in the chronicle: everyone you hold a grudge against,
  with kills each way, where you last saw them, level, race, class and guild
  when the game shows them, your own note, and a dated list of encounters.
  Waypoint to their last sighting from there.
- Add one with /warpath grudge (your target), /warpath grudge <name>, or
  "Inscribe my target" / "Inscribe a name..." in the book.
- A grudge only leaves the ledger when you forgive it (the Forgive button,
  or /warpath grudge forgive <name>). Killing one counts it as settled.
- Seeing a grudge: a banner naming the zone and your history ("killed you 4
  times, settled once"), their crest and name on the map for 15 minutes,
  and a skull on their nameplate and the target frame.
- Optional, off by default: when a player has killed you 2, 3 or 5 times,
  offer a grudge in chat (then /warpath grudge takes it) or add one
  automatically.
- New Grudges tab in the options window. /warpath grudges opens the ledger;
  /warpath grudges chat lists it in chat.

The chronicle is a book
- An open book on a leather cover: Campaigns and Grudges are tabs on its
  right edge.
- Each campaign is written up as a short paragraph ("You rode out at 08:32
  and marched 75.8 miles through Mount Hyjal..."), with its numbers and
  highlights beneath and the night's deeds and losses on the facing page.
- "Earlier campaign" and "Later campaign" buttons turn the pages.
- Share is the wax seal. Hover a deed for the enemy's level, race and guild.

The map
- Long-term memory (roads, lingering haze, flight routes) only fades on
  days you play. A weekend or a holiday away costs nothing.
- Flight routes appear after about five days of flying them, and fade out
  slowly instead of vanishing the moment they dip below that.
- Choose how long today's trail stays on the map: 2, 4, 7 (as before), 12
  or 24 hours for a stretch walked once (Map tab, or /warpath memory).

Also
- Kills and deaths record the enemy's level, race and guild when the game
  shows them (on map pins, in the chronicle and on grudges). Enemies far
  above you show as "??".
- Deaths store the killer's full name, so they can be matched to grudges.
- The options window has a fifth tab and is a little taller.

=====================================================================
3.4.1
=====================================================================

- Chronicle: names in class colours are darkened so every class reads on
  the parchment (priest white, rogue yellow and mage blue all but vanished).
  Empty highlights and the date line are darker; the date line fits on one
  line. Zone names like Mount Hyjal stay whole in headlines.
- Flight routes need about five days of flying within a couple of weeks
  before they show (was two), and are much fainter: short, sparse, muted
  dashes that ease in.
- Today's flights are thinner and softer: 45% at most (was 80%).
- Options window: tabs are drawn by Warpath along the top (the client's tab
  style was missing, which left red buttons), the background is solid, the
  footer no longer runs under the buttons, and the layout is tighter.
- The options window now opens by itself only for feature releases (3.4,
  3.5...), not fix releases like this one.

=====================================================================
3.4.0
=====================================================================

The chronicle
- /warpath chronicle opens a book of your campaigns: one per page, the last
  30 kept per character. Each page has a headline ("The Hillsbrad Skirmish
  - 9 kills, 3 deaths"), kills, deaths, best streak, miles and time played,
  six highlights (nemesis, favourite victim, bloodiest ground, longest
  streak and who ended it, grudges settled and deaths avenged, screenshot
  numbers) and every kill and death. Click one to set a waypoint there.
- A campaign is play with gaps under 2 hours: a /reload or a quick relog no
  longer splits a night in two, and empty logins don't push out real ones.
- The nemesis is the enemy you were fighting when you fell. The game doesn't
  say who landed the blow, so a third player's kill is credited to them.
- At login, a one-line headline for the campaign that just ended (option),
  or the window itself (option, off by default).
- /warpath chronicle chat prints the full report to chat instead.
- Share a campaign: the Share button, or /warpath chronicle share
  [guild|party|say|whisper <name>]. Two plain chat lines anyone can read.
- /warpath chronicle preview shows the window with sample data.

Zone records
- Entering a zone you have history in: a chat line (kills, deaths, miles,
  enemies sighted, deaths avenged, best streak there, and a tag such as
  "your deadliest zone") and a short line under the big zone name. Each can
  be turned off on its own. Again after 30+ minutes away.
- Enemy busy hours are gone from zone messages and /warpath zone.

The map
- A button in the world map's top-right corner (crossed swords): left-click
  hides or shows everything Warpath draws, right-click picks layers,
  shift-drag moves it. Recording never stops.
- Roads gild gradually: silver, warm silver, pale gold, light gold, gold.
  Well-worn gold gets a warm centre stripe, a bronze edge, a slow shimmer
  (can be turned off) and a few amber glints.
- Footsteps are about a third smaller; fresh ones are vivid with a thin dark
  rim for their first 10 minutes.
- The solid line covers your last 5 minutes and fades along its length into
  the footsteps. Fixed: it was often much shorter than intended after you
  stood still.
- Corpse runs and flights have their own layer ("runs"): spirit runs are
  dashed ice blue with a soft glow, flights thin, soft gold dashes. They fade
  along their length and stay 24 hours (1 to 48, in options).
- Flight routes you keep taking (about five separate days within a couple
  of weeks) stay on the map as faint, sparse dashes, a little stronger with
  use, fading over weeks if unused.

Commands and options
- /warpath help lists the everyday commands; /warpath help all lists
  everything, grouped. Unknown commands get a one-line hint, not a dump.
- Every on/off setting takes on or off (or toggles with neither).
- New names: map (was toggle), table (was hud), waypoint death|kill (was
  revenge / lastkill), options (was setup), layer trail / guild / runs. The
  old names still work.
- /warpath clear all asks you to confirm.
- The options window has tabs: General, Map, Screenshots, Chronicle & zones.
- The map on/off switch is shared by all your characters.

Under the hood
- Split into several files. After updating, restart the game (a /reload
  isn't enough the first time).
- Kill marks also store tag, streak and times killed (for a future
  screenshot renamer).
- Saved data upgrades itself; trail recorded before 3.4 shows as footsteps.

=====================================================================
EVERYTHING SINCE 3.2 (the 3.3 series, up to 3.3.5)
=====================================================================

Kill screenshots (replaces the PvPKillShot addon)
- Every kill in the open world gets a screenshot, or only notable kills:
  first kill of a player, grudges, avenged deaths, streaks of 3, 5 and every
  10, anyone you've killed 5+ times.
- A small caption in the shot: floating text (default), a one-line caption
  strip, or none. Banners and sounds wait until after the picture.
- Hover a blade on the map to see its screenshot number and file name.
- /warpath shot (settings, test). /killshot and /pks still work once
  PvPKillShot is removed; Warpath warns you if both are installed.

Kills and deaths
- One enemy death is one kill, however many honorable-kill credits the game
  gives for it. One death is one death, even if the game reports it twice.
- Kills only count if you were in combat (or left it in the last 3 seconds),
  so a groupmate's kill while you stood by isn't yours.
- Victims are named reliably in combat: Warpath reads what the game still
  allows, remembers players it has seen as enemies, and falls back to the
  last enemy you targeted in the past 15 seconds.
- PvP deaths are recognised in combat: an enemy you targeted recently, or a
  non-group player on a nameplate when you fall.

The map
- Silver roads: a route you've used on two separate days. Gold from about
  the third day, thicker with use. Both fade over weeks if abandoned.
- Roads have a dark outline so they show on light and dark zones.
- Today's trail is footsteps that start bright and fade over 30 minutes to a
  faint floor, held until the day's memory expires. A comet tail follows you.
- The solid line now covers only your last 2 minutes (corpse runs and flights
  keep 20).

Guild sharing
- Shares your kills as well as sightings and PvP deaths.
- Allies' kills and deaths in your zone print a chat line; sightings are
  map-only.
- Kills, deaths and test pings go out first, and the queue drains every second.
- /warpath share test sends a test ping; /warpath share status shows sent,
  refused and received counts.

Battlegrounds and arenas
- Warpath is completely off inside: no recording, banners, sounds,
  screenshots, guild messages, war table or map drawing. Detection uses several
  signals and re-checks every 5 seconds. Everything returns when you leave.

Setup and settings
- A setup window opens once after each install or update with the key
  settings; /warpath setup reopens it. Escape doesn't count as seen; if it
  fails to open, the error is printed in chat.
- These settings are shared by all your characters.
- /warpath debug shows what Warpath can and can't see.

VERSION BY VERSION
=====================================================================

3.3.5
- Footsteps fade by time since you last walked there: bright and slightly
  larger at first, easing to a faint floor over 30 minutes, then held until
  the day's memory expires. Walking a stretch again makes it bright again.
- The solid line covers your last 2 minutes (was 20). Corpse runs and
  flights, which leave no footsteps, keep 20 minutes.
- The setup window lists everything new since 3.2.

3.3.4
- Battlegrounds and arenas: Warpath now checks several signals (instance
  type, battleground and arena state, zone PvP type, PvP match events) and
  re-checks every 5 seconds. Inside, it records nothing, shows no banners or
  caption, plays no sounds, takes no screenshots, sends and receives nothing
  from the guild, hides the war table, draws nothing on the map, and holds the
  setup window until you leave.
- The same victim is now ignored for 10 seconds after a kill (was 30), so a
  player rezzed and killed again counts twice.
- /warpath debug says whether Warpath thinks you're in a battleground.

3.3.3
- Kills are counted per enemy death, not per honorable-kill credit. Credits
  arriving within two seconds are one kill unless they name a different dead
  enemy, and the same victim is never counted twice within 30 seconds (10 from 3.3.4). This
  feeds everything downstream: kill marks, streaks, screenshots, nemesis
  counts, shared kills.
- Deaths are counted once per death even if the game reports it twice.
- /warpath debug shows credits received vs kills counted this session.

3.3.2
- Shared sightings no longer print a chat line; they show on the map only.
  Allies' kills and deaths in your zone still do.

3.3.1
- Kill captions now name the victim. In combat the game hides whether your
  target is hostile or dead, and that check used to give up; Warpath now
  works with what it can read, remembers players it has seen as enemies, and
  falls back to the last enemy you targeted (within 15 seconds).
- PvP deaths are recognised in combat too: an enemy you targeted recently, or
  a non-group player on a nameplate when you die, now counts.
- Guild sharing now includes your kills (crossed blades on allies' maps for
  two hours). Kills and deaths always get a chat line for allies in the same
  zone; sightings stay at most one a minute.
- Kills, deaths and test pings jump the send queue, and it drains every second.
- /warpath share test sends a test ping; /warpath share status shows messages
  sent, refused and received this session.
- /warpath debug shows what Warpath can and can't see.
- The setup window: pressing Escape no longer counts as having seen it, and
  if it fails to open the error is printed in chat.
- Same message format as 3.3.0, so guildmates on 3.3.0 still get your
  sightings and deaths (they need 3.3.1 to see kills and test pings).

3.3.0
- Kill screenshots, folded in from PvPKillShot. Every honorable kill in the open
  world by default, or notable kills only (first kill of a player, grudges,
  avenged deaths, streaks of 3, 5 and every 10, anyone killed 5+ times).
- A small caption in the shot: floating text (default) or a one-line caption
  strip, or none. Banners and sounds wait until after the picture.
- Hover a blade on the map to see its screenshot number and file name.
- Kills now only count if you were in combat (or left it in the last 3
  seconds), so a groupmate's kill while you stood by no longer counts as yours.
- Silver roads: a route you have used on two separate days. Gold from about
  the third day, as before.
- Roads are outlined so they show on light and dark zones; today's trail is
  now footsteps instead of a solid line.
- A setup window opens once after each install or update. /warpath setup
  reopens it. Its settings are shared by all your characters.
- /killshot and /pks still work (unless PvPKillShot is still installed).
  Warpath warns you if PvPKillShot is running: delete it to avoid two
  screenshots per kill.

3.2.0
- Two memories: short memory fades within the day, long memory builds one
  mark per day and fades over weeks.
- Lingering haze, roads only from travel, /warpath memory and /warpath roads.

3.1.0
- Etched gold roads, soft heat texture.

3.0.0
- Heat map, enemy sightings, shared war map, grudges, avenged, zone standing,
  chronicle, replay.
