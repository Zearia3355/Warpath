Warpath changelog

=====================================================================
EVERYTHING SINCE 3.2 (the 3.3 series, up to 3.3.5)
=====================================================================

Kill screenshots (replaces the PvPKillShot addon)
- Every kill in the open world gets a screenshot, or only notable kills:
  first kill of a player, grudges, avenged deaths, streaks of 3, 5 and every
  10, anyone you've killed 5+ times.
- A small caption in the shot: floating text (default), a one-line caption
  strip, or none. Banners and sounds wait until after the picture.
- Hover a blade on the map to see its screenshot number and file name.
- /warpath shot (settings, test). /killshot and /pks still work once
  PvPKillShot is removed; Warpath warns you if both are installed.

Kills and deaths
- One enemy death is one kill, however many honorable-kill credits the game
  gives for it. One death is one death, even if the game reports it twice.
- Kills only count if you were in combat (or left it in the last 3 seconds),
  so a groupmate's kill while you stood by isn't yours.
- Victims are named reliably in combat: Warpath reads what the game still
  allows, remembers players it has seen as enemies, and falls back to the
  last enemy you targeted in the past 15 seconds.
- PvP deaths are recognised in combat: an enemy you targeted recently, or a
  non-group player on a nameplate when you fall.

The map
- Silver roads: a route you've used on two separate days. Gold from about
  the third day, thicker with use. Both fade over weeks if abandoned.
- Roads have a dark outline so they show on light and dark zones.
- Today's trail is footsteps that start bright and fade over 30 minutes to a
  faint floor, held until the day's memory expires. A comet tail follows you.
- The solid line now covers only your last 2 minutes (corpse runs and flights
  keep 20).

Guild sharing
- Shares your kills as well as sightings and PvP deaths.
- Allies' kills and deaths in your zone print a chat line; sightings are
  map-only.
- Kills, deaths and test pings go out first, and the queue drains every second.
- /warpath share test sends a test ping; /warpath share status shows sent,
  refused and received counts.

Battlegrounds and arenas
- Warpath is completely off inside: no recording, banners, sounds,
  screenshots, guild messages, war table or map drawing. Detection uses several
  signals and re-checks every 5 seconds. Everything returns when you leave.

Setup and settings
- A setup window opens once after each install or update with the key
  settings; /warpath setup reopens it. Escape doesn't count as seen; if it
  fails to open, the error is printed in chat.
- These settings are shared by all your characters.
- /warpath debug shows what Warpath can and can't see.

=====================================================================
VERSION BY VERSION
=====================================================================

3.3.5
- Footsteps fade by time since you last walked there: bright and slightly
  larger at first, easing to a faint floor over 30 minutes, then held until
  the day's memory expires. Walking a stretch again makes it bright again.
- The solid line covers your last 2 minutes (was 20). Corpse runs and
  flights, which leave no footsteps, keep 20 minutes.
- The setup window lists everything new since 3.2.

3.3.4
- Battlegrounds and arenas: Warpath now checks several signals (instance
  type, battleground and arena state, zone PvP type, PvP match events) and
  re-checks every 5 seconds. Inside, it records nothing, shows no banners or
  caption, plays no sounds, takes no screenshots, sends and receives nothing
  from the guild, hides the war table, draws nothing on the map, and holds the
  setup window until you leave.
- The same victim is now ignored for 10 seconds after a kill (was 30), so a
  player rezzed and killed again counts twice.
- /warpath debug says whether Warpath thinks you're in a battleground.

3.3.3
- Kills are counted per enemy death, not per honorable-kill credit. Credits
  arriving within two seconds are one kill unless they name a different dead
  enemy, and the same victim is never counted twice within 30 seconds (10 from 3.3.4). This
  feeds everything downstream: kill marks, streaks, screenshots, nemesis
  counts, shared kills.
- Deaths are counted once per death even if the game reports it twice.
- /warpath debug shows credits received vs kills counted this session.

3.3.2
- Shared sightings no longer print a chat line; they show on the map only.
  Allies' kills and deaths in your zone still do.

3.3.1
- Kill captions now name the victim. In combat the game hides whether your
  target is hostile or dead, and that check used to give up; Warpath now
  works with what it can read, remembers players it has seen as enemies, and
  falls back to the last enemy you targeted (within 15 seconds).
- PvP deaths are recognised in combat too: an enemy you targeted recently, or
  a non-group player on a nameplate when you die, now counts.
- Guild sharing now includes your kills (crossed blades on allies' maps for
  two hours). Kills and deaths always get a chat line for allies in the same
  zone; sightings stay at most one a minute.
- Kills, deaths and test pings jump the send queue, and it drains every second.
- /warpath share test sends a test ping; /warpath share status shows messages
  sent, refused and received this session.
- /warpath debug shows what Warpath can and can't see.
- The setup window: pressing Escape no longer counts as having seen it, and
  if it fails to open the error is printed in chat.
- Same message format as 3.3.0, so guildmates on 3.3.0 still get your
  sightings and deaths (they need 3.3.1 to see kills and test pings).

3.3.0
- Kill screenshots, folded in from PvPKillShot. Every honorable kill in the open
  world by default, or notable kills only (first kill of a player, grudges,
  avenged deaths, streaks of 3, 5 and every 10, anyone killed 5+ times).
- A small caption in the shot: floating text (default) or a one-line caption
  strip, or none. Banners and sounds wait until after the picture.
- Hover a blade on the map to see its screenshot number and file name.
- Kills now only count if you were in combat (or left it in the last 3
  seconds), so a groupmate's kill while you stood by no longer counts as yours.
- Silver roads: a route you have used on two separate days. Gold from about
  the third day, as before.
- Roads are outlined so they show on light and dark zones; today's trail is
  now footsteps instead of a solid line.
- A setup window opens once after each install or update. /warpath setup
  reopens it. Its settings are shared by all your characters.
- /killshot and /pks still work (unless PvPKillShot is still installed).
  Warpath warns you if PvPKillShot is running: delete it to avoid two
  screenshots per kill.

3.2.0
- Two memories: short memory fades within the day, long memory builds one
  mark per day and fades over weeks.
- Lingering haze, roads only from travel, /warpath memory and /warpath roads.

3.1.0
- Etched gold roads, soft heat texture.

3.0.0
- Heat map, enemy sightings, shared war map, grudges, avenged, zone standing,
  chronicle, replay.
